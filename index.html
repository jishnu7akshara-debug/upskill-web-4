§05

Questions
answered.

Q.01Do I need to know how to code?

For Full-Stack, Data and XR — no. For AI Engineering and Cloud we run a free 2-week bridge course first. Cybersecurity needs basic Linux comfort.

Q.02What's the weekly time commitment?

On-site tracks run Mon–Fri, 10:00–17:00. Weekend and live tracks need about 15–18 hours a week.

Q.03Is there an entrance test?

A 40-minute logic assessment plus a short conversation with a mentor. It's about fit and motivation, not prior knowledge.

Q.04How does placement support work?

After your capstone, the career desk runs mock interview loops and refers you to partner companies until you sign. We don't guarantee jobs.

Q.05Can I pay in instalments?

Yes — zero-cost EMI for up to 12 months and an income-share option on select tracks, subject to approval.
